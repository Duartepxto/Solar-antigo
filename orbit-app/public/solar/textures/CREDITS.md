# Planetary surface maps

Author: Solar System Scope / INOVE.
Original collection: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Download mirror: https://github.com/elymas/solar-simulator/tree/main/public/textures

Included files: `2k_sun.jpg`, `2k_earth_daymap.jpg`, `2k_earth_nightmap.jpg`, `2k_earth_clouds.jpg`, `2k_saturn.jpg`, `2k_saturn_ring_alpha.png`, `2k_mars.jpg`, `2k_moon.jpg`.

The image pixels are unmodified. Jarvis adds provenance metadata. The homepage samples the Earth, Saturn, Mars and Moon maps for fragment colors, then applies its own palettes, three-dimensional geometry, lighting and shader effects; the Sun is procedural. Small interface previews also use the maps. These are Solar System Scope's artistic maps, partly based on NASA imagery; they are not a claim of scientific photometric accuracy. The About screen links to the original collection and license.
